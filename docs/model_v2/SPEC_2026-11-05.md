# Model v2 experiment specification

**Frozen 2026-11-05, before any v2 comparison was run.** Anything decided after
seeing v2 results is marked as such in `RESULTS.md`. The v1 model, its tables
(`reports/tables/stage1_*`, `exp_*`) and its stimuli (`docs/milestone2/arm_c_stimuli.csv`)
are preserved unchanged.

**Honesty about contamination.** v1 out-of-sample results (ridge 7.46%, ablations,
regimes) were inspected before this spec was written, and they shaped what is tried
here. Every v2 comparison is therefore **exploratory**, not confirmatory. The only
data nobody has seen are the 7 CFTC weeks after the pinned cutoff
(2026-09-15 .. 2026-10-27). That is too few to confirm anything and is not used.

## 1. Question

Question 1 only: *explain this week's positioning adjustment* from market
conditions. Not forecasting a future adjustment (question 2), and not
positioning → future returns (question 3).

## 2. Target and information set

- `s_t` = leveraged-fund net contracts / open interest, UST 10Y, futures-only TFF.
- **Primary target** `y_t = s_t - s_{t-1}`.
- **Robustness target** `y_alt_t = (N_t - N_{t-1}) / OI_{t-1}`. It strips out
  movements that come from the denominator. The two targets have different scales,
  so they are compared on R² against their own benchmarks and on the correlation
  of their predictions. They are never compared on raw MSE, and the primary target
  is not swapped for whichever scores higher.
- **Expected level:** `s_hat_t = s_{t-1} + y_hat_t`. The residual
  `s_t - s_hat_t = y_t - y_hat_t` is an unexpected *adjustment*, conditional on
  last week's position. It is not an unexplained share of the outstanding position.
- **Information set** (see Amendment 1 below: the "pre-Friday nowcast" sentence is
  withdrawn): market data through the close of snapshot day *t* (yields,
  VIX, futures prices, funding rates), and CFTC data through snapshot *t-1*
  (published the Friday before *t*). This is a **same-week nowcast**. Everything
  is observable by about Wednesday after *t*, before the CFTC releases `s_t` on
  Friday. It is not a forecast of future positioning. Caveats: FRED values are
  published with a one-day lag, and the weekly Fed balance sheet (`TREAST`, a
  Wednesday level) is as-of-filled from the previous Wednesday, which was
  published on Thursday.
- **Revisions:** the live CFTC endpoint was compared cell by cell with the pinned
  panels on 2026-11-05. There were 0 revised cells in all four contracts. FRED
  yield series are not vintage-tracked here, which is a stated limitation.

## 3. Candidates (fixed list)

| id | model | features |
|---|---|---|
| B0 | zero change | none |
| B1 | expanding historical mean change | none |
| B2 | AR(1), OLS with intercept | `y_{t-1}` |
| M3 | ridge, **corrected** (scaler inside every inner split) | the 40 v1 "extended" features |
| M4 | ridge, corrected | M3 + trend block |
| M5 | ridge, corrected | M4 + economic block |
| M6 | fused-ridge distributed lag (Boos–Grob adaptation) | 250 weekly-differenced daily ZN returns |

M3–M6 each run with an **expanding** window and a **5-year (260-week) rolling**
window. Minimum training is 260 weeks, refit every 13 weeks (as in v1).

**Trend block (M4):** `ret_26w`, `ret_52w`; three EWMA crossovers of daily log
price, (fast, slow) half-lives of (8, 24), (16, 48) and (32, 96) trading days,
each divided by trailing 63-day realised volatility (vol-scaled); and the weekly
change in each crossover (3), because the target is an adjustment. That is 8
features, all causal.

**Economic block (M5):**
- Leveraged-fund gross long, short and spreading / OI, all lagged 1 week (3).
  "Spreading" is the CFTC's offsetting long/short within a category. It is **not**
  a measure of cross-maturity curve trades.
- Log leveraged-fund long and short trader counts, lagged, plus the lagged change
  in the short count (3).
- Leveraged-fund 10Y share of net DV01 across 2Y/5Y/10Y/30Y, and a curve tilt
  ((10Y+30Y) − (2Y+5Y)) / Σ|·|, both lagged (2). **The DV01 is an
  approximation:** fixed per-contract DV01s of $38 / $42 / $63 / $150, roughly
  face value × a typical CTD duration. Raw contract counts are not equal rate risk.
- Treasury realised-volatility change `d_rvol_4w` (1).
- Interactions `net_over_oi_lag1 × rvol_4w` and `funding_spread × vix` (2).

That is 11 features.

**M6, adapted from Boos & Grob (2023), eqs. 5 and 7:**
`y_t = Σ_{i<250} β_i (r_{p_t-i} - r_{p_{t-1}-i}) + ε`, where `p_t` is the
trading-day position of snapshot *t*. Using the actual previous snapshot instead
of a fixed 5-day offset handles holiday weeks exactly. The penalty is
`γ²Σ(β_i-β_{i+1})² + γ²β_{249}²` (uniform smoothing, their base case), solved
as augmented least squares with an unpenalised intercept. Daily returns enter in percent (x100), so the γ grid spans weak to strong smoothing relative to XᵀX (unit choice fixed here, before results).

Differences from the paper:
- `γ` is chosen by inner time-series CV, not LOOCV.
- Our target is a net/OI change, not contracts.
- We refit every 13 weeks, not weekly.
- The asset is one Treasury contract, not 23 commodities.

Their ~40% R² is **not** a benchmark for us.

**Search spaces (fixed):** ridge `alpha ∈ logspace(-2, 4, 13)`; fused ridge
`γ ∈ logspace(-1, 3, 9)`; inner `TimeSeriesSplit(4)` over the whole pipeline
(`GridSearchCV`). There are no other hyperparameters.

## 4. Audit items run alongside (diagnostic, not candidates)

- v1 ridge with the scaler fitted before the inner split, against M3, to size the
  audit-A fix.
- v1 GBM (random 20% early-stopping holdout) against GBM with `early_stopping=False`
  and fixed `max_iter=150` (audit B).
- Ablation of `d_log_oi` from M3. It shares the target's denominator mechanics.
- The `futures_cash_spread` feature is renamed `futures_yield_residual` in v2. It
  is a futures-return residual after the CMT-yield move (CTD/roll/convexity/timing
  noise), not a cash–futures valuation basis.

## 5. Evaluation

- **Common weeks:** every candidate is scored on the intersection of the weeks
  all candidates predict. Coverage of each candidate is reported separately.
- **Metrics:** OOS R² against B1 (mean) and B0 (zero, which equals R² of the
  reconstructed level against a random walk); MAE; RMSE; bias; prediction SD;
  Mincer–Zarnowitz intercept and slope; residual autocorrelation at lags 1–2;
  corr(residual, actual).
- **Uncertainty:** the loss differential (squared error) against M3, with a
  Newey–West HAC t-statistic (4 lags) and a stationary-bootstrap 90% CI (mean
  block 13, 1000 draws, seed 20260918).
- **Predeclared splits:** eras 2013–16 / 2017–19 / 2020–22 / 2023–26.
  - Stress: VIX at *t* above (below) the 67th (33rd) percentile of VIX in all
    *prior* weeks.
  - Extreme positioning: `s_{t-1}`'s percentile within the prior 260 weeks is
    ≤ 10% or ≥ 90%.
  - All thresholds use prior data only.
- **Ablations:** diagnostics only, not causal attribution.

## 6. Selection rule (predeclared)

The recommended v2 model is the candidate with the lowest common-week MSE,
**only if** it beats M3 with HAC one-sided p < 0.10 **and** its bootstrap 90% CI
for the MSE reduction excludes zero. Otherwise **retain M3** (the corrected v1
ridge). Ties go to the simpler model. With six comparisons against M3, a p of
0.10 is weak evidence, and the write-up says so.

**The v1 gate is retained and disclosed.** corr(residual, raw) ≤ 0.9 was
pre-registered and failed (0.957). It is not replaced after seeing results. v2
reports the same statistic beside R².

## 7. Known limitations

- No CTD prices, conversion factors or term repo, so no genuine basis valuation
  (cf. Glicoes et al. 2024).
- MOVE is not available. Realised ZN volatility stands in for rate volatility.
- Primary-dealer positions (NY Fed) have series breaks in 2013, 2015, 2022 and
  2024. They are feasible but deferred.
- Form PF / OFR Hedge Fund Monitor data are quarterly with a publication lag.
  They are not usable weekly without vintage dates.
- An FOMC/data-release calendar would need hand maintenance, so it is deferred.
- The ZN=F continuous series is not back-adjusted. Roll-week returns showed no
  outliers (roll-week SD 0.35% vs 0.37% otherwise).

## Amendment 1 (2026-11-05, after results; does not change any model or number)

The claim above that the model is a nowcast "observable by about Wednesday after
*t*, before the CFTC releases `s_t` on Friday" is **withdrawn**. The feature
`d_log_oi` is the change in **this week's CFTC open interest**, which comes from
the same Friday report as the target. Exchange-reported open interest is
published daily by CME, but we have no timestamped source showing it equals the
CFTC futures-only figure at the time. Until that is shown, the model is described
as a **retrospective same-week conditional estimate**: given what is known once
the week's report is out, what change in positioning would ordinary market
conditions have led us to expect? This is all the LLM experiment needs.
Pointed out by an external review of the v2 code.
