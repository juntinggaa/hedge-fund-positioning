Hedge funds hold a massive net short position ($2.25\text{M}$ contracts). This isn't necessarily a bearish economic call on higher yields; rather, it reflects hedge funds selling overvalued futures while buying cash Treasuries and financing them in the repo market to capture tiny price discrepancies (the basis trade).
