The table highlights the classic structural divide in the 10-Year Treasury futures market:

* **Leveraged Funds are heavily net short (-247,740 contracts / -8.05% of OI):** Hedge funds and CTA strategies were positioned for higher Treasury yields (falling bond prices). * **Asset Managers are net long (+201,434 contracts / +6.55% of OI):** Institutional real-money accounts (pension funds, mutual funds, insurance companies) act as the primary counterparties, holding long positions to duration-match liabilities or absorb safe-haven demand.
